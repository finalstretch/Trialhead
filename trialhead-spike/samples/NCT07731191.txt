Inclusion Criteria:

* Male or female subjects aged more than 18 years at the time of signing the informed consent form;
* Subjects with MCI or SCD who, at the time of their first visit, did not have a clinical diagnosis of dementia (MMSE ≥ 24);
* Smartphone user.

Exclusion Criteria:

* Age younger than that stated in the inclusion criterion;
* Inability to understand.
